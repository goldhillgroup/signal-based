1. First impressions free

1

The login page

Open the URL logged out. Look at the right-hand panel.

Good: a short headline, three numbers (57 → 44 → 13) with arrows between them, one line of text. You should be able to take it in without reading a paragraph. Wrong: a wall of text, or the numbers not fitting on one row.
2

Every page, empty

Sign in. Visit all five: Overview, Signal Radar, Enrichment, All Leads, Settings.

Good: every page loads with zeros and an explanation. "No searches yet, run your first one above" rather than a blank space. Wrong: a blank page, a spinner that never stops, or an error. Empty is expected; broken is not.
3

On your phone

Open the same URL on your actual phone. Tap into the search box and an API key field.

Good: no sideways scrolling anywhere, buttons easy to hit, and the page does not zoom in when you tap a field. Wrong: the page zooms on focus and will not zoom back. That was a real bug; worth confirming it is gone on a real device.

2. The guardrails free

4

Search with no credit

Before topping up, try to run any search.

Good: it refuses and tells you the amount and what to do. No folder is created. Wrong: a folder appears, or it runs and produces an empty result. An empty folder reading as "no leads" when the truth is "out of credit" is the single worst failure this product can have.
5

The form will not let you do something silly

Open "set it up manually". Deselect all four states. Then try to switch on the weekly harvest with nothing selected.

Good: Search greys out and tells you which input is missing. The harvest refuses to switch on empty. Wrong: a disabled button with no explanation of why.

3. A real search costs money

6

Run one small search

Top up OpenRouter first. Then: one vertical, one state, target 10. Roughly $0.45.

Watch the loading screen. This is the part I could not test, because showing it needs a live search.

Good: a percentage that climbs, a counter (3 / 10 companies), and a three-step indicator showing Finding → Reading → Judging with the current one lit and a pulsing dot. It is normal for it to go back to Finding; the pipeline loops. Wrong: a bar that never moves with no stage shown, or a stage that never changes.

You can close the dialog. The search keeps running on the server.

7

Judge the results yourself

This is the real test. Open the folder and click into a company marked with a signal.

Read the quote it captured, then open the company's actual website and check it.

Good: the founder and the next-generation person are both really named on that page, and both are really still involved. The quote is real, not paraphrased. Wrong: the founder retired or died, the two people are brothers rather than father and son, or nobody is actually named. Those are the three traps the system is built to avoid, so any of them is worth telling me about.

Then click the Rejected tab and read a few reasons. They should be specific ("only one generation is on the leadership page"), not generic.

8

Run the same search again

Identical query, second time. Another ~$0.45.

Good: different companies from the first run. It should move to a different city and phrase the question differently. Wrong: the same companies, or "ran out of companies to check" after only a handful. Both were real bugs and both are fixed, so either one returning matters.

4. Contacts and the spreadsheet costs money

9

Enrichment, and the choice it gives you

Go to Enrichment. Each folder offers two buttons.

Good: both show a count and a price, e.g. 2 signals ~$0.10 and all 12 leads ~$0.60. Pick the cheap one first.

Then check All Leads: the emails should appear, marked valid or risky.

10

Download it

All Leads picks a folder by default, never "everything". Hit download.

Good: the file contains only that folder's leads, and the button said how many.

5. The weekly harvest free to arm

11

Switch it on and check it sticks

Settings → Weekly harvest. Pick verticals, states, a day. Switch on, then reload the page.

Good: still on after the reload. Wrong: it reverts to off. That was a real bug (a cache serving stale settings) and it is fixed, so a recurrence matters.

You also need something pinging /api/cron/weekly daily with the secret, or it will never fire. Vercel Cron does this.

6. Things worth trying to break

Type nonsense in the search box ("find me 9999 crypto companies on Mars"). It should ask a sensible question, not crash.

Sign out and back in. Everything should still be there.

Leave mid-search, close the tab, come back. The search should still be running or finished, not lost.

Look for an em-dash (the long — character) anywhere in the app. There should not be one.

What I already verified

54 automated checks, 22 interface checks against an empty account, 30 error-path checks confirming every failure gives a readable reason, and 5 real end-to-end searches costing $0.25 in total. No console errors on any page. Production is live and reading its settings correctly.
